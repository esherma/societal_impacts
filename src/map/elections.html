Why this can't answer the question yet

  • Unofficial, partial count. The 2026 election-night count covers roughly half of expected ballots, compared with about 68% for the 2024 snapshot it's compared against. Late WA ballots lean younger and more Democratic, so swing estimates will move.
  • Exposure ≈ education. Tract exposure correlates with BA+ share. The sign flips once education is controlled, which is exactly what confounding looks like. A noisy education control leaves residual confounding that the exposure term can absorb.
  • Ecological inference. These are places, not voters. A tract full of exposed workers that swung doesn't mean the exposed workers swung.
  • Measured ≠ perceived. The index measures occupational exposure, not whether voters believe AI threatens their jobs. That's a survey question; see the 2028 plan in Claude_Proposals/impact_atlas/04_election_overlay.md.
  • One county. The county is about 78% Democratic. Its range of exposure and partisanship isn't representative.
